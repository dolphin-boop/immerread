Why Language Models Are Becoming a New Reading Interface

01

For most of the web's history, reading tools have treated text as a finished surface. A page could be saved, highlighted, or searched—but its language remained fixed.

01

在互联网历史的大部分时间里，阅读工具都把文本视为一个已经完成的界面。网页可以被保存、标注或搜索，但它所使用的语言始终固定不变。

02

Large language models change that relationship. Instead of replacing the article, they can create a parallel reading layer that follows the author's structure paragraph by paragraph.

TERM · parallel reading layer
02

大语言模型改变了这种关系。它不必取代原文，而是可以创建一个平行阅读层，逐段跟随作者原有的文章结构。

术语 · 平行阅读层
03

The useful unit is not the entire document, but the paragraph. Keeping each translation beside its source preserves context, makes errors easier to spot, and lets the reader move at their own pace.

03

真正有用的单位不是整篇文档，而是段落。将每段译文放在原文旁边，既能保留上下文，也更容易发现翻译错误，并让读者按自己的节奏阅读。
