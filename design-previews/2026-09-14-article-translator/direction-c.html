The Shape of Attention in an Age of Machines

By Eleanor Hart · 8 min read

Every new tool changes not only what we can do, but what we notice. The clock taught workers to divide the day; the camera taught travelers to frame the world.

Artificial intelligence is beginning to make a similar demand on attention. Its answers arrive quickly, yet the value of those answers still depends on the patience of the person asking.

The question is no longer whether machines can produce language. It is whether we can remain careful readers of what they produce.

机器时代，注意力将呈现怎样的形态

埃莉诺·哈特 著 · AI 翻译

每一种新工具，不仅改变我们能做什么，也改变我们会注意什么。时钟教会劳动者划分一天，照相机教会旅行者框取世界。

人工智能也开始对注意力提出类似要求。它的回答来得很快，但回答是否有价值，仍取决于提问者是否愿意耐心思考。

问题已不再是机器能否生成语言，而是面对它生成的内容，我们能否继续做审慎的读者。
