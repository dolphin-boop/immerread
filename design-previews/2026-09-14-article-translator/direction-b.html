English / SourceEN-US
01
Reliable AI systems are not defined by a single model benchmark. They emerge from the interaction between models, retrieval layers, validation rules, and human oversight.
02
A production pipeline should treat model output as an untrusted proposal. Grounding, schema validation, and confidence thresholds turn that proposal into a dependable result.
03
Latency also shapes user trust. Streaming the first useful sentence is often more valuable than returning a polished answer several seconds later.
04
The final design principle is observability: every response should be traceable to its prompt, context, model version, and evaluation outcome.
