Over the past year, we've worked with dozens of teams building large language model agents across industries.

过去一年，我们与来自不同行业的数十个团队合作，共同构建大语言模型智能体。

The most successful implementations weren't using complex frameworks. Instead, they were building with simple, composable patterns.

最成功的实现并没有采用复杂框架，而是使用简单、可组合的模式进行构建。

We distinguish between workflows, where systems orchestrate models through predefined code paths, and agents, where models dynamically direct their own processes and tool usage.

我们区分两类系统：工作流通过预定义代码路径编排模型；智能体则由模型动态决定自身的执行过程与工具调用方式。

When building applications with LLMs, we recommend finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity when needed.

使用 LLM 构建应用时，我们建议先选择最简单的可行方案，只在确有需要时增加复杂度。
